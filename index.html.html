Example case study

Understanding sales performance and growth opportunities

Illustrative scenario: a retailer wants to understand changes in revenue and identify which product and regional segments deserve closer attention.

SQLPower BI
01

Business problem

Leadership needs a consistent view of sales performance across time, product categories, and regions.

02

Analysis

Clean and validate transaction data, use SQL to aggregate performance, and compare trends across business dimensions.

03

Dashboard & findings

Build KPI cards and trend visuals for revenue, order volume, average order value, and category contribution. Document findings only after validating the data.

04

Recommendations

Investigate segments with declining performance, review high-contribution categories, and establish a recurring KPI review cadence.
